Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

1. Information We Collect

  • Site content: Names, logos, audio files, and layout data you upload.
  • Usage data: Basic logs (IP address, timestamps) for security purposes.
  • No personal information is collected unless you provide it voluntarily.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely to:

  • Provide the Service (render your sites, serve your audio).
  • Debug issues and prevent abuse.
  • Improve the Service.

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

All site data and audio files are stored on Cloudinary, a global CDN provider. Authentication tokens are stored in your browser's localStorage.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your data with third parties, except:

  • With Cloudinary (for storage/serving).
  • With Sentry (for error tracking, if enabled).
  • If required by law.

5. Data Retention

Site data is retained as long as your account is active. You can delete sites at any time from the dashboard.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

7. Contact

For privacy concerns, contact corporateaffairs@um.edu.my.
